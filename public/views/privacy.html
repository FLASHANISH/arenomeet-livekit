Privacy Policy

ArenaMeet is built to collect as little as possible. There are no accounts, and the app does not record your calls.

Last updated: 21 November 2026

What ArenaMeet is

ArenaMeet is a video meeting app that runs in your web browser. When you join a room, your audio, video and screen share travel live between the participants through the app's video service. ArenaMeet itself does not store your calls.

What we collect

Almost nothing. Specifically:

  • No accounts. You do not create an account, and ArenaMeet does not ask for your email address, phone number or password.
  • Display name. The name you type when joining a room is shown to the other participants in that room, and only while you are in the room.
  • No call recordings. ArenaMeet does not record audio, video or chat, and it does not keep transcripts of your meetings.

How calls work

Live audio and video are relayed by the app's video service (LiveKit Cloud) so participants can see and hear each other. That service processes media in real time to deliver the call and does not keep recordings of it. Chat messages in a room are delivered live to the other participants and are not stored on the ArenaMeet server.

To let you join a room, the server creates a short-lived join token (valid for 6 hours). The token carries only your room name and the display name you chose.

What is stored in your browser

  • Display name. Your last used display name is saved in your browser's local storage so the join form can pre-fill it next time. You can clear it by clearing your browser data.
  • Session identifier. A random identifier is kept in your browser's session storage for the duration of your visit so the room can tell participants apart. It is discarded when you close the tab.

ArenaMeet does not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers.

Room links

Rooms are open to anyone who has the room link — there is no guest list and no password. Treat a room link like an invitation: only share it with people you want in the meeting. Anyone in the room can see the display names of the other participants.

Your choices

You can join with your camera and microphone off, and turn them on only when you want to. Your browser asks permission before any camera or microphone access, and you can revoke it at any time in your browser settings. Leaving a room ends your participation immediately.

Contact

Questions about this policy should go to the operator of the site where you use ArenaMeet — that is the person or organisation that deployed this copy of the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
